How the Algorithm Works

DAA concept
1

Define state: dp[i][c] stores the maximum benefit using the first i products with capacity c.

2

Don't select: keep dp[i-1][c].

3

Select: use benefit + dp[i-1][c-space] when the product fits.

4

Take maximum: compare selecting vs skipping.

5

Backtrack: move from the last DP cell to identify exactly which products were selected.

Time: O(n × C)   •   Space: O(n × C)
